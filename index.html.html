💬 Sentence Structures – Mẫu câu

🎤 Speaking Practice – Câu hỏi tương tác

Chọn câu hỏi bất kỳ. Học sinh trả lời miệng bằng câu đầy đủ.

Do you like Christmas? Why?

Gợi ý mở rộng câu trả lời

Because...
I can spend time with my family.
Also,...
I enjoy decorating the Christmas tree.
Usually,...
I exchange presents with my friends.

Pair work – Hội thoại theo cặp

Student A: Do you like Christmas?
Student B: Yes, I do. I love Christmas!
Student A: What do you usually do at Christmas?
Student B: I decorate the Christmas tree and exchange presents with my family.
Student A: What do you want for Christmas?
Student B: I want a new bicycle. What about you?
Student A: I would like a teddy bear.
Student B: That sounds great! Merry Christmas!
